The problem

Project-finance investors can't see their real AI exposure

  • Lenders and syndicators now fund data centers, GPU facilities and lease/offtake-backed debt
  • Deals are interlocking and often circular: chipmakers fund labs and neoclouds that buy their chips
  • Shocks are multiplying: 715 local moratoria in effect, state grid pauses, and governance shocks at hub companies
  • Today: site tracker + bill tracker + filings + terminal, joined by hand in spreadsheets; slow, and stale as soon as policy moves
